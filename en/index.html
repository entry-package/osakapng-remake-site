ABOUT OSAKAPNG

Bringing the fun closer.

OsakaPNG is an Osaka-based talent team operated by PACkage Inc. Growing from esports competition, the team shares games, streaming, music and other forms of entertainment, including the work of VTubers and YouTubers.

The team began as PNG esports in 2018 and became OsakaPNG in 2023. Through online streams and in-person events, it connects with local communities. Its Takoyaki Penguin mascot combines Osaka's takoyaki with the penguin behind the PNG name.
